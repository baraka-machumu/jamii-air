Draft texts for the Google Play and App Store listings (BR-BN-01, Plan K1.2). Nothing is published
yet: the store accounts wait for Plan 5.0 ([08](../docs/08-release-readiness-tracker.md) D4).
Describe only what the build in the store can do; add Stories, The Ocean, Udaku rooms, Chat and

Package `tz.jamiiair.app` (D-14: the app ID does not change with the name). Icon: `assets/icon.png`.

## English

**App name** (30 max): Kidili

**Short description** (80 max): Share photos and videos with your people across Tanzania and Kenya.

**Full description:**

> Kidili is where you share your day with the people who matter: friends, family and your
> community across Tanzania and Kenya.
>
> - Post photos (up to 10 at once) and short videos, with a caption and #hashtags
> - Follow people and see their posts in your Home feed
> - Like, comment and reply
> - Make your account private and choose who follows you
> - Use it in English or Kiswahili, in light or dark mode
>
> Kidili is for adults 18 and over. Report or block anyone, any time.
